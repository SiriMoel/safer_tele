Aims to make teleportation safer by avoiding liquids (so you won't be teleported directly into lava or other dangerous materials) and introducing other safety features.

Currently makes Teleportatium, Unstable Teleportatium, enemy-inflicted teleportation, and the Teleportitis Perk safer.

All this mod's features can be enabled/disabled and adjusted in the mod settings.
There's also the option to enable a short-duration Slow Falling effect that is applied when you teleport to further improve safety.
(it does make teleportation much safer, but it's disabled by default because it isn't in vanilla Noita)
