Aims to make teleportation safer by avoiding liquids (so you won't be teleported into lava or other dangerous materials) and introducing other safety features.

Currently makes Teleportatium, Unstable Teleportatium, the Master of Teleportation, and the Teleportitis Perk safer.

All this mod's features can be toggled and customised in the mod settings to adjust teleportation safety & other features.
There's also the option to enable a short-duration Slow Falling effect that is applied when you teleport to further improve safety.
(it does make teleportation much safer, but it's disabled by default because it isn't in vanilla Noita)
